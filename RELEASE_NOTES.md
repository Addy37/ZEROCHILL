# ZEROCHILL 7.5: The Experience Update

New sources. Smarter search. Your content, your way. Explore five ShitTok feeds, discover Pawchive and Erome, and make your Library your own. NO LIMITS. ALL CONTENT.

## ShitTok

- Choose from five independent feeds: Chaos, Straight, Gay, Trans and Groups. Each remembers its own place and playback state. Feed switching prioritizes returning videos and keeps slow sources from blocking the rest of a feed.
- Erome videos join Straight, Gay and Trans. The ShitTok source preference is independent of Erome search.
- See the playback provider beside the creator name. Open an eligible creator gallery or Erome album by swiping or tapping its shortcut, then return to your clip and playback position.
- Horizontal clips open in a dedicated fullscreen player. Tap to show or hide the controls, double-tap either side to seek, drag the timeline, or choose a playback speed. The video stays in place while fullscreen and returns to the same spot in the feed.
- Landscape playback keeps the soft ambient backdrop. ShitTok also prioritizes playback buffers when returning to a feed.

## Sources, Spotlight and Shows

- Pawchive brings photos and GIFs to OnlyFap, with discovery shelves and matching Bunkr media where available. The OnlyFap preference picker combines the Trans choices while preserving saved selections.
- Search Erome directly in Spotlight. Erome albums show photos in their original proportions, with video thumbnails and duration badges. Its saved preference applies to Erome search and album browsing.
- Spotlight presents one list across providers. Bunkr and Erome load more results as you scroll. Empty results and connection errors have distinct states, and recent searches remain available.
- Shows search ranks matches across providers and includes Kaotic search. Continue Watching uses better-framed preview images.

## Library, collections and creators

- Find saved photos and videos, Watch Later, history and downloads in the updated Library. Saved Library media can start playing while its album context loads.
- Create and manage custom collections of favorite photos and videos. Collections display artwork from their contents and are included in new ZEROCHILL backups. In ShitTok and Shows, tap the bookmark to save directly to Watch Later or a collection.
- Favorite Creators has an adaptive grid and a draggable alphabet. The index previews the letter while you drag through populated sections.
- Library destinations use consistent headers and inline search. Grid layouts adapt to the available width.

## Galleries, communication and Settings

- Gallery thumbnails prepare ahead in bounded batches. Artwork stays in place through the reveal, unavailable photos are handled after a retry, and creator actions in mixed galleries follow the selected item.
- Notifications, direct messages, feedback, public profiles, and account and security screens share clearer headers and controls.
- The searchable Settings hub groups related destinations and brings appearance, playback, content preferences and updates within easier reach. The compact More menu gives direct access to its six destinations and shows unread messages and active downloads.

## Stability and compatibility

- Fixed a startup crash that could affect signed-in users when Firebase was temporarily unavailable.
- Busy message threads now load the most recent messages inside the existing 500-message window.
- What’s New appears after the one-time source access screen when it is shown during an ordinary upgrade launch.
- Restoring a backup checks saved source links before applying changes. Backups from 7.4 remain compatible with 7.5 collections.

## Startup and appearance

- New installations get a four-step cinematic introduction with the full animated ZEROCHILL mascot. Existing installations continue on the normal launch path and see the 7.5 highlights.
- The redesigned splash follows the selected app accent. The refreshed launcher stays cyan by default and supports Android’s themed icon setting.
- The OLED black palette, seven app accents and liquid glass styling carry through the updated screens.

## Updating

Install **ZeroChill.apk** over your existing ZEROCHILL installation to keep your saved data. Version 7.5.0 uses Android version code `7005084` and the established production package and signing identity.
