# ZeroChill 4.2.1

ZeroChill 4.2.1 fixes OnlyFap Discover instability and startup-wizard behavior.

## OnlyFap Discover

- Fixed Discover shrinking to one or two creators or disappearing as the other shelves finish loading.
- Discover now waits for the final New, Hot, and Popular results before appearing.
- Short candidate pools refill from deeper Fapello pages to show up to 16 eligible creators.
- Random creator selection, duplicate protection, and cached fallback remain active.
- New Creators, Hot, Popular, creator galleries, and custom avatars keep their existing behavior.

## Startup wizard

- Fixed the wizard being skipped after clearing app storage on an updated install.
- Replaced the blinking page transition with an overlapping slide and crossfade.
- Removed the extra hide-and-reappear animation between setup pages.

## Compatibility

The update keeps the same application ID and production signing identity. Favorites, creator merges, custom avatars, history, downloads, backups, feedback, and saved settings remain compatible. No backend or remote-source configuration changes are required.

Supports an in-place upgrade from ZeroChill 4.2.0.
